# LANTERNFALL — Keep the Last Light

An original top-down arena survival game for make.fun's best-game-01 bounty.
You are Wren, the last lantern-keeper of a dusk garden. Burn the shadows with
ember shots, rescue drifting fireflies, take a lantern upgrade after every
wave, and survive 10 waves — past the Gloam Brute at wave 5 — to face the
Dusk Queen and bring the dawn.

- 10 waves, 6 shadow kinds, 2 bosses, 9 stackable upgrades (3-card draft)
- Combo multiplier, firefly rescues, ember pickups, best score in localStorage
- WebAudio-synthesised sound effects and generative lantern melody (M to mute)
- Particles, screen shake, hit-stop, lantern aura, pause, victory stars

Play: `node serve.ts`, open http://localhost:8080. WASD move, mouse aim, hold
click to shoot, Shift dash, P pause, R restart. No network request, no build
step — static files under `game/`.

Screenshots are real renders of this code (`?shot=title|play|boss|victory`).
